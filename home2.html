How Our Nonprofit Works

The science, community, and stewardship model.

We operate as a transparent non-profit hub connecting city master planners, certified arborists, regional native nurseries, and local volunteers.

1. Ecological Diagnosis

Every planting location undergoes deep soil testing, solar modeling, and utility checks to select resilient native species.

2. Community Ownership

We train local volunteer Tree Captains, fostering long-term neighborhood pride and hands-on environmental education.

3. Guaranteed 3-Year Care

Weekly hydration, organic mulch donuts, and biometric growth audits ensure 92%+ of saplings mature into towering canopy trees.

Arboricultural Blueprint

The 5 steps of tree planting and maintenance.

Science-backed care guarantees healthy root architecture and lasting tree vigor.

01
Step 1 • Survey

Soil & Site Diagnostics

Mapping urban heat islands, certifying 811 utility clearance, and analyzing soil pH and drainage.

02
Step 2 • Excavation

Wide Basin Pit & Flare Placement

Digging a saucer-shaped pit 2-3x wider than root ball. Placing root flare 1-2 inches above grade.

03
Step 3 • Planting

Native Backfilling & Settling

Backfilling with unamended native soil and light tamping with water to eliminate all air pockets.

04
Step 4 • Protection

Mulch Donut & Slow Drip Bag

Applying 3 inches of aged organic mulch kept 3 inches from trunk bark, plus 15-gal hydration bag.

05
Step 5 • Aftercare

3-Year Monitored Maintenance

Weekly summer watering, stake removal after season one, structural pruning, and biometric logs.
