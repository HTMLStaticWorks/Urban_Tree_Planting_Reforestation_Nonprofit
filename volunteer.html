The Experience

What to expect on your first planting day.

No prior gardening or forestry experience needed. We provide all tools, guidance, and refreshments for a rewarding day outdoors.

Step 01

Welcome & Briefing

Gather at 8:00 AM for coffee, safety orientation, and planting demonstration by certified arborists.

30 Minutes
Step 02

Gear & Tool Up

Receive high-quality work gloves, shovels, mulch buckets, and native saplings tailored for your group.

Tools Provided
Step 03

Hands-On Planting

Dig soil beds, position sapling root balls, water thoroughly, and add protective mulch rings together.

2.5 Hours
Step 04

Celebrate & Track

Enjoy a group lunch, take team photos, and log your hours into the Volunteer Portal dashboard.

Lunch & Log
