Operational Framework

How our nonprofit works.

Urban Forest bridges the gap between city agencies, academic ecologists, local nurseries, and residents to create self-sustaining urban canopies.

Stage 01

GIS Satellite & Soil Diagnostics

We prioritize urban neighborhoods with low canopy cover, high thermal heat index, and poor air quality. Soil scientists test permeability, organic content, and compaction before ground is broken.

Site Selection
Stage 02

Native Nursery Propagation

We contract with regional ethical nurseries to grow native tree species from ethically harvested local seeds. This ensures trees possess genetic adaptations suited for local droughts and pests.

Climate Genetics
Stage 03

Grassroots Mobilization

We train volunteer Tree Captains, host neighborhood workshops, and supply all tools, mulch, and safety gear. Planting day becomes a celebration of collective ecological ownership.

Community Action
Stage 04

3-Year Active Maintenance

The most critical phase: we irrigate trees with 15–20 gallons weekly during summer, perform seasonal structural pruning, adjust stakes, and record biometric growth in our open impact portal.

Guaranteed Care
Arboricultural Standards

The 5 steps to planting and maintaining trees.

Every single one of our 148,620+ trees follows this precise arboricultural lifecycle to guarantee maximum root establishment and canopy health.

01
Step 1 • Planning

Site Assessment & Underground Clearance

We test soil texture, water drainage, and shade tolerance. 811 utility clearance is certified before any digging begins.

✔ Soil pH & Drainage Test
02
Step 2 • Preparation

Wide Basin Excavation & Flare Inspection

Digging a saucer-shaped pit 2 to 3 times the width of root ball. Root flare is located and placed 1-2 inches above ground level to prevent collar rot.

✔ Circling roots pruned
03
Step 3 • Planting

Backfilling with Native Unamended Soil

Refilling with original native soil prevents drainage interface barriers. Soil is lightly tamped in stages with water to eliminate air pockets.

✔ Zero air pockets
04
Step 4 • Protection

Mulch Donut & Slow Drip Irrigation

A 3-inch ring of organic woodchips is spread outward to the drip line, keeping mulch 3 inches clear of trunk bark. A 15-gallon slow-release bag is attached.

✔ 15-gal slow-hydration
05
Step 5 • Maintenance

3-Year Active Pruning & Canopy Auditing

Scheduled weekly watering, organic weeding, stake adjustment after year 1, and seasonal corrective pruning by certified arborists.

✔ 92.4% survival rate
